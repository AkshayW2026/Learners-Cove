Library periods

Class library periods

Your class visits the library with your teacher. Some periods are for browsing and borrowing; others are for a research task, a database tutorial or a skills lesson with the librarian.

Bring the books you have finished, and be ready to choose the next one.

Teachers

Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

What if I lose a book?

Tell a librarian as soon as you notice. We would much rather know than have you worry about it.

Can I keep a book longer?

Renew it online from My library account, or ask at the desk. If someone is waiting for it we will let you know.

How do I return a book after hours?

Use the drop box for after-hours returns.

How do I borrow an eBook or audiobook?

Sign in with your school email and password. Two digital titles at a time, and no separate login.

About Learners Cove

Learners Cove, the Academy library, sits at the centre of the Academy's nine-acre campus in the Financial District, overlooking the lake. Opened with the school in 2022, it serves readers from Early Years to Grade 10 across an IB continuum.

Our work is simple to state and harder to do: put the right resource in a young reader's hands at the moment they are curious, and teach them how to find the next one themselves.

Borrowing rules, copyright guidance and the acceptable-use policy are in the library handbook.
